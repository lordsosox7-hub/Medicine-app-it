📱 Screen Context:

- Device: iPhone 14 Pro (mobile viewport)
- Layout direction: Right-to-left (Arabic)
- Grid system: 8pt spacing system
- Safe areas respected (top notch & bottom bar)

- Accent colors:
  - Green (normal/healthy)

- Cards: Pure white with subtle shadow (elevation 2–4)

🔤 Typography:

- Font: Tajawal / Cairo (modern Arabic UI font)
- Clear hierarchy:
  - Heading (bold)
  - Subheading (medium)
